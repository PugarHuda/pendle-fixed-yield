Pendle Fixed Yield

A PayBox plugin that lets your agent lock a fixed rate on idle stablecoins, ETH, BTC, HYPE or tokenized T-bills by buying Pendle Principal Tokens. It handles the whole lifecycle (find, explain, quote, deposit, track, exit and roll over) and verifies every route before PayBox asks you to approve.

Source on GitHub · built for the MoonPay PayBox Hackathon

"Put 25 USDC somewhere it earns a fixed rate until February." The agent finds the best market, explains its risks, quotes the rate, then proposes an exact-amount approval and the deposit. At maturity it rolls the PT into the next one in one transaction.

Tools

find_fixed_yieldsEvery active market on 8 chains in one call. Shows fixed vs variable APY, premium, liquidity, TVL and risk flags.

quote_fixed_yieldHow much PT you get, the effective APY, USD value now and at maturity, price impact, fee, and balance and approval checks.
propose_fixed_yield_depositAn exact-amount approval, then the verified router deposit.
get_fixed_yield_positionsPT holdings across chains, read on-chain and valued in USD, with a suggested roll-over target.
propose_fixed_yield_exitRedeem after maturity, or sell early at the market price.
propose_fixed_yield_rolloverMoves an old PT into a later PT of the same asset in one callAndReflect transaction.

Why you can trust the proposal

  • It never signs. It only returns unsigned io.paybox/transactionProposal calls, and PayBox simulates each one and asks you before signing.
  • Pendle's API is treated as untrusted. The plugin decodes the calldata itself and only proposes a route that targets Pendle Router V4 0x8888…F946, with you as receiver, the requested market and the exact amount.
  • Roll-overs are checked call by call. Each nested call must match, and the reflector must be the one in Pendle's official deployments for that chain.
  • Approvals are for the exact amount, never unlimited. Mainnet USDT is reset to 0 first.
  • Risky trades are refused or flagged. It refuses trades over the price-impact limit, and flags low liquidity, short maturity, new markets, volatile underlyings, suspicious spreads, and points that go to YT rather than PT.

Chains

Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, HyperEVM, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, Monad and Plasma. Each one was verified with a PayBox portal simulation.
