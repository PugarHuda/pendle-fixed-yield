How it works

“Put 25 USDC somewhere it earns a fixed rate until February.”

The agent finds the best market, explains its risks, quotes the rate, then proposes an exact-amount approval and the deposit. At maturity the PT redeems back to you, like the coin coming down the steps above, or rolls into the next maturity in one transaction.

Safety

Why you can trust the proposal

  • It never signs. It only returns unsigned io.paybox/transactionProposal calls, and PayBox simulates each one and asks you before signing.
  • Pendle's API is treated as untrusted. The plugin decodes the calldata itself and only proposes a route that targets Pendle Router V4 0x8888…F946, with you as receiver, the requested market and the exact amount.
  • Roll-overs are checked call by call. Each nested call must match, and the reflector must be the one in Pendle's official deployments for that chain.
  • Limit orders are rebuilt and re-hashed. Every field of Pendle's generated order must match your request (rate within 1e-9, expiry before maturity, empty permit), and the order is hashed on-chain by Pendle's Limit Router before it is proposed.
  • Approvals are for the exact amount, never unlimited. Mainnet USDT is reset to 0 first.
  • Risky trades are refused or flagged. It refuses trades over the price-impact limit, and flags low liquidity, short maturity, new markets, volatile underlyings, suspicious spreads, and points that go to YT rather than PT.
